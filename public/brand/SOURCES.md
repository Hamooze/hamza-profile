# Brand marks

Original brand geometry is retained. Neon color and glow are applied by the
portfolio's CSS, with the white background of Barmous masked at render time.

- GitHub, WhatsApp, Instagram: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons), CC0; see `simple-icons-LICENSE.txt`.
- LinkedIn: [Font Awesome LinkedIn In](https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/blob/7.x/svgs/brands/linkedin-in.svg), Fonticons, Inc., CC BY 4.0; see `fontawesome-LICENSE.txt`. The attribution comment is preserved in the SVG.
- Barmous: original `services/web/public/barmous-logo.png` from the owner's Barmous repository, B and crescent mark.
- Nemu: original `apps/web/public/brand/nemu-stepped-mark.png` from the owner's Nemu repository, stepped N mark.
